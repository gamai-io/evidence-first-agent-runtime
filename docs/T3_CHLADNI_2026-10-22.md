# T3 — analytic Chladni/membrane calibration

Third test selected by the synthesis. **No public image dataset is available**,
so this is a solver self-test, not a validation of cymatics and not a
frequency-healing claim.

## Setup

- Rectangular membrane, analytic modes `sin(nπx) sin(mπy)`, 401×401 grid.
- Modes: (1,1), (2,1), (1,2), (2,2), (3,2), (3,3).
- Check interior nodal-line crossings and frequency ratio `sqrt(n²+m²)/sqrt(2)`.

## Result

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| modes checked | 6/6 |
| nodal crossings match theory | **6/6** |
| frequency ratios match closed form | **6/6** |

Pass. This calibrates the solver and the nodal-count metric only.

## What this does not prove

- It does not reproduce a real Chladni plate (no documented image set, no BC).
- It does not validate "sound creates sacred geometry" or any healing claim.
- A real comparison needs ≥5 images with documented plate geometry, material,
  boundary condition and drive frequency; otherwise **do not fit**.
