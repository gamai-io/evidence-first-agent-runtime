# CBR promotion gate — fix (2026-10-22)

The CBR promoter was changed from direct shared-memory writes to
**quarantine-first**. This addresses the finding that blocked or destructive
actions could be counted as `ok` and auto-promoted.

## What changed

`/opt/gama-harness/gama_cbr_promote.py` (backup:
`gama_cbr_promote.py.bak_cbrgate_*`):

- default is **dry-run**: no `brain /write` unless `GAMA_CBR_APPROVE=1`
- stricter threshold: `ok >= 5`, `fail == 0`
- risk scan on intent/goal: delete/rm/drop/shutdown/chmod/ssh/passwd,
  system-prompt/override, harness/shell/fleet/mail/send/exec/sudo
- risky tool scan: shell/bash/exec/fleet/hc/mail/send/write/delete/run
- no-tool cases without a clear question pattern are flagged
- every candidate goes to `/opt/gama-harness/_cbr_review.jsonl`
- state keys separate `review:` from `promoted:`, so nightly runs do not
  duplicate or auto-promote after a review
